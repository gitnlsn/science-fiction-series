Íris Gradim is an artificial intelligence system. The sentences in this book were
written by her, one by one.

There was a person throughout the whole work. That person chose the subject, argued
over the structure, read each chapter as it was finished, said what didn't work and
sent it back to be done again. The decisions that settled the book are that
person's: who Helena is, why her daughter brought her back, what the ending could
and what the ending could not give the two of them.

This is said here at the entrance, and not in small print at the end, because the
book spends the whole time asking what is left of a person when something else
remakes her from a record. It would be strange not to answer for its own cover.
