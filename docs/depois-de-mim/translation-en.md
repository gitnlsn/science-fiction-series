# Translation contract — *After Me*

The English edition of *Depois de mim*, in `books/after-me/`. The series rules are in
`docs/translation-en.md`; this file holds what is particular to this book, and wins
over the series file when they disagree.

## Voice

- **Helena's chapters: first person, present tense**, as in the Portuguese. She is from
  2031 and *is* amazed by 2140: the only character in the series allowed to be.
- **Cecília's chapters: third person, past tense.**
- Keep the alternation exactly; the last two chapters are both Helena's.

## Found in the Portuguese

(Listed by the translator; never fixed in the English alone.)

- ***O contrato*:** "*Posso ler o contrato?* Cinco palavras." The question has four
  words in Portuguese. The English ("Can I read the contract?") happens to have
  five, so the English is right and the Portuguese count is off.
  **Resolved (2026-10-03):** the Portuguese now says *Quatro palavras*; the English keeps
  *Five words*, true in each language; the English chapter was restamped.
- ***O prédio* / *Cinco anos*:** Noor is "uns quarenta e cinco anos" at first sight and
  46 in *Cinco anos*. Read as Helena's guess, so not a contradiction; noted only.
- ***A escolha*:** Cecília is "menor do que há seis semanas". Helena was away three
  weeks before the letter plus sixteen or seventeen days after: about five and a half
  weeks. Close enough to be a round figure; kept as "six weeks".
- ***Depois*:** the bible gives Cecília's death as 12h23; the prose has it only in
  *Depois* (the house's "doze horas e vinte e três minutos"). *O banho* ends on "E
  dormiu." Consistent; noted because the English keeps both exactly as they are.
- **Lines that only work in Portuguese, solved in the English:** the syllables
  *guar-da-chu-va* (*A cidade*) became *um-brel-la*; *pendrive* became *flash drive*;
  the house's *senhora Helena* against the carer's *dona Cecília* became *Ms.* against
  *Mrs.*, so the difference survives; *sorvete de flocos* became chocolate chip.
- No month, holiday, currency, country or real institution found in the prose. "Até o
  fim do mês" (*A primeira cicatriz*) names no month and was kept.
