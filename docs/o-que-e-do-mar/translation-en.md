# Translation contract — *What Belongs to the Sea*

The English edition of *O que é do mar*, in `books/what-belongs-to-the-sea/`. The series
rules are in `docs/translation-en.md`; this file holds what is particular to this book,
and wins over the series file when they disagree.

## Voice

- **Third person, past tense, close on Joana**, single point of view. Her habit of doing
  the arithmetic before feeling is the voice: keep every number and every *fez a conta*.
- **The title line.** *O que é do mar* is said in the hearing (*É do mar.*) and is the
  theme. **What belongs to the sea** / **It belongs to the sea.** The chapter of the same
  name keeps the book's title.

grande maré* (the second, a year later, grander) are two different titles: **The Big

night is **the big tide** throughout.

## Found in the Portuguese

(Listed by the translator; never fixed in the English alone.)

- **A month in the prose.** *O que é do mar*: "Os dias da ressaca de maio, os do
  enterro." (*maio*, against the series rule). The English follows the glossary: *the
  days of the second surge*. The Portuguese wants the same fix.
- **Where the slide rule is.** *A primeira jangada* has Joana fetch the slide rule from
  the upper-city apartment and bring it down; *A conta* has her using it on the
  platform. *Thalassa* then finds it "na gaveta da cozinha do apartamento, onde sempre
  tinha estado". Either she took it back up (unsaid), or *Thalassa* should find it on
  the platform. Translated as written.
- **Sixteen or fourteen years.** In *O estaleiro* Amaro says the wall "segurou dezesseis
  anos"; the bible has it built and its gates raised fourteen years before (sixteen is
  the Council vote). Possibly deliberate (Amaro counting from the decision); translated
  as written.

**Resolved (2026-10-03), Portuguese fixed first, English carried and restamped:** the
month (*a segunda ressaca*); the slide rule is on the platform table, not in the
apartment; Amaro says the wall held **fourteen** years (built ~2076).
