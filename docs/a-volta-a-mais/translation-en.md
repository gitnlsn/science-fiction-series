# Translation contract — *The Extra Turn*

The English edition of *A volta a mais*, in `books/the-extra-turn/`. The series rules are
in `docs/translation-en.md`; this file holds what is particular to this book, and wins
over the series file when they disagree.

## Voice

- **Iara narrates in the first person, present tense**, single point of view.
- **Otávio's sayings** are fixed sentences: each must be the same English every time it
  appears, and each is listed below before it is used.
- **Fair play:** every physical fact and number that solves something is on the page
  before it is used. Keep every number exact (with a decimal point: `2,9 kg` → `2.9 kg`);
  never round, never convert out of metric.

**The title.** *A volta a mais* is the extra turn Otávio taught Iara to put inside the
bowline, the one that holds nothing: *Amarra como se fosse voltar. Não é para você. É
para quem vem.* In English the knot is a **bowline** and the turn is **the extra turn**
(never *round turn*, *the extra loop*). The burial line in *Terra*: *dou a volta a mais.
Por dentro. A que não segura nada.* → **I add the extra turn. On the inside. The one that
holds nothing.**

## Found in the Portuguese

(Listed by the translator; never fixed in the English alone. The English follows the
Portuguese in every case below.)

- **The travel time Station → Post.** *O que não cai* and *A trava* give **52 h 35 min**
  for 15,786 km at 300 km/h; the arithmetic is **52 h 37 min** (15,786 ÷ 300 = 52.62 h).
  *A trava*'s own clock agrees with 52 h 37: released 10:02 Friday, docked 14:39 Sunday.
  Fair play: one of the two numbers should change.
- **How old Otávio's sum is.** He wrote the ruled box "in the middle of the second
  month." *Megapascal* (on 05-10): "Faz quase três" — correct. *O dilema* (05-15): "A conta
  dele é de quatro meses atrás." *O que não cai* (05-16): "Dois por dia era a resposta de
  dois meses atrás." Three different ages for the same page.
- **C-114 at the stop.** In *O que não cai*, the emergency stop halts "O C-114, lá embaixo,
  na Âncora, que ia descer vazio" — but C-114 is at the Station, in the transfer yard,
  where Iara left it; and in *Terra* it is back on the cargo deck. "lá embaixo, na Âncora"
  looks like a slip for "no pátio".
- **"Cem anos de canto do cabo"** (*O que ele deixou*, Otávio, in Iara's first year, and
  echoed in *A dose*): the cable was about ten years old then and is twenty at the book's
  present. It may be Otávio's hyperbole; if so it is the only one of its kind.
- **"seis vezes em quatro anos"** (*Megapascal*, *O relatório*): Otávio's table has
  **five** measurements (2107, 2108, 2109, and two in 2110); the sixth point is Iara's.
  "Seis pontos" for the curve is right; "ele mediu, seis vezes" is not.
- **The seal on Otávio's door** (*Cem por cento*, 03-21): it reads *inspecionado — dia 14*,
  and Iara says the agency came "Três dias atrás, enquanto eu estava no Posto". The 14th
  is seven days before 03-21 (and she was at the Post then); three days before is the 18th,
  when she was already descending. One of the two should change. (*A suspeita* confirms
  the 14th.)
- **"Ele subiu com o meu código, sem pedir, a um homem que não é da Operadora pagando a
  minha amiga por fora"** (*Bia*): the "a" reads as a slip (for "com"); translated as
  "with a man who isn't from the Operator paying my friend on the side".
- **"o comandante da Estação, uma mulher"** (*O que não cai*, *A trava*): masculine
  article for Laís. Neutral in English ("the Station commander").
- **The tilde.** Iara twice describes Otávio's handwriting by *o til torto*; English has no
  tilde, so the English uses the dot on the i (*Still*, *Tension*). Not an error; noted
  because it is the one place the English had to substitute an image.

**Resolved (2026-10-03), Portuguese fixed first, English carried and restamped:**
- Station → Post is **52 h 37 min** everywhere (bible and timeline too).
- Otávio's calculation is **about three months** old in *O dilema* and *O que não cai*.
- C-114 is **in the transfer yard** at the emergency stop.
- The agency's seal (*dia 14*) was put on **seven days** before Iara opens the door.
- Otávio measured **five** times in four years; his calculation has **five points**, and
  the curve has six only with Iara's.
- *Bia*: Otávio went up *no escalador de um homem que não é da Operadora*.
- Laís is ***a* comandante**.
- Left as written, deliberately: *cem anos de canto do cabo* (Otávio's hyperbole).
